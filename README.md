**Ember Duel** — a duelling card battler built for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty, $50 in USDC. You and a rival each have 20 life. Mana grows every turn; cards strike, guard, mend and draw across four suits — Flame ▲, Frost ◆, Leaf ●, Bolt ★ — told apart by colour and shape, never by text alone. Play two cards of one suit in a turn and the second lands with Resonance +2. The rival announces its next move before it plays, so you can read it and counter with a shield or a heal.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click a card (or press 1–5, then Enter) to play it, End Turn to hand over to the rival, Esc to pause, M to mute. No build step, no network, no dependencies: plain ES modules and WebAudio synth.
